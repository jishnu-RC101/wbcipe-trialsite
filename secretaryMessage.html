Message from the Organizing Secretary of W.B.C.I.P.E

From the desk of the Organizing Secretary

I, on behalf of the Organizing Committee, welcome you all to 36th edition of WBCIPE Cultural and Athletic Meet, 2025. After two and half years we are again meeting at Berhampore, Murshidabad in the 36th edition of the WBCIPE Cultural and Athletic meet with only 12 institutes that in 2009 we organized the 20th edition of the said meet at Berhampore with 18 institutes.

“West Bengal Committee of Institutes of Physical Education” is now 37 years old. A sea change has taken place in the last four decades in Physical Education and allied disciplines. However, the vision and mission of the committee has largely remained unchanged which is to be relevant, dynamic, and contemporary.

WBCIPE is now one of the eminent professional committees in India with over 100 members. This has representation from different Colleges and University Departments of Physical Education. We have members from Government colleges, Aided colleges, Private colleges and University Departments. The members of the society are spread over from rural institutions to advanced cities like Kolkata.

WBCIPE Cultural and Athletic Meet is one of the effective reflections of its Cultural, Academic, Physical and Social contributions. Not only does it allow us to meet, greet, and eat but it also provides a unique forum for the exchange of ideas, offers, and opportunities.

Let us together infuse new enthusiasm in young, not-so-young, and veteran personalities to make Physical Education and Sports a relevant, dynamic, contemporary, and most sought-after discipline.

Thanking you,

Dr. Subhabrata Kar

Secretary, WBCIPE
